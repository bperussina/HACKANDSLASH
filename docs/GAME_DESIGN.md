# The Demolisher

Hack-and-slash action RPG. You are a god of violence that Hell and Heaven both tried to use. Now you hunt both of them.

This is the creative bible. Combat systems and Three.js rules live in the constitution and the playable-arena spec. If those conflict with feel, the constitution still wins on engineering. This file wins on fantasy.

## The fantasy

This is not a soldier who gets strong. This is a locked weapon that wakes up already terrifying, then gets worse.

The Doom Slayer fantasy, turned into an ARPG:

- **Shoot** a hole in the pack
- **Hack** through whatever is still standing
- **Rip** the big one apart
- **Loot** the corpse like a trophy
- Walk into a new land. That land ends.

Every session answers one question: **are the evil forces afraid of me yet?**

Early: they swarm you.  
Mid: Hell starts running.  
Late: Heaven sends its best, and you tear the wings off anyway.

If a system does not make the Demolisher feel more unstoppable, it does not belong.

---

## Who the Demolisher is

Hell wanted a weapon. Heaven wanted a weapon. They poured power into the same being, then locked it away when they could not control it.

The game begins the moment the lock breaks.

The Demolisher does not pick a side. Does not make speeches. Does not accept a crown. Other things talk *at* the Demolisher. The Demolisher answers by ending them.

Look: a god-shaped wrecking figure. Not a knight, not a marine. Armor that starts as ugly Hell iron and, over the campaign, cracks with stolen gold and broken halos. By the end the body looks like both realms glued a god together and it hated them for it.

Power is visible. Leveling is not a quiet +3. The silhouette gets more cracked, more glowing, more wrong.

---

## Why Hell, then Heaven

Hell is loud, ugly, honest. It wants to eat the world.

Heaven is worse. Gold, clean, certain it is right. Its angels do not scream. They **erase**. They call slaughter “order.”

So the war has two halves:

1. **Hell first** — chaos, fire, bone, things that charge.
2. **Heaven later** — light, marble, wings, things that judge.

Hell learns fear. Heaven still thinks it can put the Demolisher back in the box. That is the joke. That is the power trip.

## Camera

The player can toggle, any time they are in control:

- **Over-the-shoulder** — see the Demolisher. This is the god-fantasy camera. Guns, executions, and “I am the monster in the room” live here.
- **Isometric** — see the pack. This is the ARPG camera. Waves, loot on the ground, and reading space live here.

Default for a new run: over-the-shoulder.  
Both cameras MUST keep the hero, incoming attacks, and hit range readable. Atmosphere never hides a windup. Toggle is a control, not a cutscene.

## Combat identity

Every fight should cycle the same crunch:

1. Gun opens the pack.
2. Blade finishes the close ones.
3. Execution rips a champion.
4. Loot hits the floor.

Weapons are legendary from the first hour, then mutate. Builds are different ways to be the same silent god:

- Shotgun and fist
- Blade storm
- Walking artillery
- Chain that yanks angels out of the sky

Executions are the Demolisher’s “voice.” Glory-kill energy. Short, brutal, readable. They drop better loot.

---

## Loot look

Visual language tracks the war.

**Hell iron (early)**  
Ugly. Bone, rust, spikes, black iron, leather that looks cooked. Shotguns like furnace doors. Blades like butcher tools.

**Stolen Heaven gold (mid-late)**  
Marble, gold, hard light, halos used as hardware. Guns that fire hymns. Blades with clean edges that should not exist on a killer.

**Cursed holy (endgame)**  
The mix. Gold cracked with ash. Halos turned into saws. A cannon that coughs brimstone and white fire together. Nothing looks blessed. Everything looks *taken*.

Loot is trophies, not clutter. If it does not look like something ripped off a throne, it is not a legendary.

## The journey

Doom-style land progression. Each land changes the creatures and the loot.

1. **The Wound** — mortal world where the lock broke. Ash in the sky. First demons. Tutorial that already feels like a massacre.
2. **The Ash Courts** — outer Hell. Imps, bone dogs, screaming mobs. Guns + blades school.
3. **The Blood Cities** — deep Hell. Armored demons. Fire that thinks. First real bosses.
4. **The Pit King** — Hell’s throne. You kill it. Hell tries to crown you. You refuse.
5. **The Gate Between** — dead white-and-black. Neither realm. The game turns here.
6. **The Choir** — lower Heaven. Perfect soldiers. Halos like blades. Enemies that shine and punish.
7. **The High Host** — winged siege-things, living statues, weapons made of law.
8. **Both Thrones** — the last court. The secret comes out. The final fight.

---

## Enemy families

Same bloodline, new nightmare, the way Doom’s roster grows.

**Hell**

- Mutts → armored hell-knights → siege beasts
- Imps → warlocks → pit princes
- The Pit King (Hell’s public throne)

**Heaven**

- Watchers → seraphs → thrones (wheeled burning things)
- Halo soldiers → choir-blades → unfold-angels (look like people until they don’t)
- The Judge (Heaven’s public throne)

Heaven fodder is not “pretty imps.” They hold formation. They heal. They call your violence unclean while they erase you.

---

## Final boss: someone from both thrones

The Pit King and the Judge were never two rulers.

They were **one being with two faces**, playing both sides, feeding the war, using the Demolisher as a shared weapon, then locking it when the weapon learned to hate the hand that held it.

Name: **the Diarch**.  
Title: **Both-Crowned**.

Left half is Hell’s king. Right half is Heaven’s judge. One body. Two voices. They talk over each other. They still think they own you.

Campaign shape:

1. You kill the Pit King. It laughs like that was only a mask.
2. You kill the Judge. The halo cracks and the other face is still underneath.
3. At Both Thrones, the masks come off. The Diarch stands up wearing both crowns and tries to put the Demolisher back in the box.

The last fight is not “pick a side.” It is demolish the lie that there were ever two sides.

---

## How you get more powerful

ARPG meat on a Doom skeleton.

- Levels change the body, not just the numbers.
- Weapons mutate along the Hell-iron → Heaven-gold → cursed-holy track.
- Skill choices are playstyles (gun, blade, rip, storm), not homework.
- You start strong. The campaign is *monster to myth*, not zero to hero.

The Demolisher should feel like a boss that escaped into the player slot.

---

## Tone

- Silent protagonist. Other characters may plead, boast, pray, or scream. No answer.
- No redemption arc. No “maybe Heaven was right.”
- Funny is allowed if it is cruel and short (Hell trying to crown you). Never jokey.
- The player is here to shoot, hack, slash, rip, and tear.

Protect this sentence in every feature:

> The Demolisher walks in. The throne does not survive.
